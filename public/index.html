What am I looking at?

Each dot is a UAP (Unidentified Aerial Phenomena) sighting reported by US military or intelligence personnel — Air Force, Navy, NRO, and others. The Department of War released these files; the agency tag tells you who filed the original report.

This is not a surveillance map. It shows where US personnel observed something they couldn't identify, mostly inside US training ranges and over the oceans where the Navy operates.

A red border on a dot means the file is redacted — so the location may be vague ("Pacific Ocean") because the precise coordinates are still classified.

Click any dot or card to see the original PDF / video on war.gov.
